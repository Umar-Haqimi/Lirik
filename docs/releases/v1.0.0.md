# Lirik 1.0.0

Lirik is a KDE Plasma 6 panel widget that shows what is playing, controls playback and displays
**time-synced, word-by-word lyrics**, both in the panel and in a side panel of the full view.

It is a fork of [PlasMusic Toolbar](https://github.com/ccatterina/plasmusic-toolbar) by Claudio
Catterina (GPL-3.0). Everything upstream does still works. With lyrics switched off Lirik behaves like
upstream, and it installs alongside it under its own plugin id, `my.umarh.lirik`.

## Highlights

- **Panel karaoke.** The current lyric line replaces the song text in the panel. A wipe follows the sung word
  for word-synced lyrics, or pans across the line for line-synced ones. During long instrumental gaps the title
  comes back and the lyrics return when singing resumes.
- **Side panel.** Scrolling lyrics with per-word highlight, overlapping vocals, a gap indicator, click a line
  to seek. Left, right or hidden, with a toggle button and a footer for credits, sync kind, timing offset,
  other matches and refetch.
- **Two providers.** [LRCLIB](https://lrclib.net) and [Unison](https://unison.betterlyrics.org), in either
  order. Word-synced results win over line-synced, line-synced over plain. A match more than 10 s off the
  track length is never shown. Unison lyrics are credited to Unison and to the person who submitted them.
- **Local cache.** Found and not-found lookups, per-track offsets and choices are remembered on your machine,
  so replayed tracks need no network.
- **Careful lookups.** Identifying headers, debouncing, at most two requests per provider in five seconds,
  `Retry-After` honoured, requests cancelled when the track changes.
- **Light on resources.** One timer scheduled for the next change. Nothing runs while paused or hidden.
- **Translated** into Spanish, French, Italian, Dutch, Brazilian Portuguese, Turkish, Ukrainian and Simplified
  Chinese.

## Install

Needs KDE Plasma 6.0.4 or newer. Developed and tested on Plasma 6.8 / Qt 6.12 (Arch, Wayland).

Download `lirik-1.0.0.plasmoid` from this release, then:

```sh
kpackagetool6 -t Plasma/Applet -i lirik-1.0.0.plasmoid
```

Right-click the panel, choose **Add Widgets**, search for "Lirik" and drag it onto the panel or desktop.
Open the widget settings and look at the **Lyrics** page to pick providers, panel behaviour and the side
panel position.

To upgrade later: `kpackagetool6 -t Plasma/Applet -u lirik-<version>.plasmoid`.
To remove: `kpackagetool6 -t Plasma/Applet -r my.umarh.lirik`.

## Known limitations

- Lyrics are matched on title, artist, album and duration (plus a YouTube id when the cover-art URL reveals
  one). Exact Spotify or YouTube id lookups are planned.
- Panel lyrics are not shown in vertical panels. The widget still works there.
- Translations and romanization carried by the lyrics are parsed but not shown yet.
- The per-player checklist in `docs/MANUAL_TEST.md` (Spotify, YouTube Music, local players) was run by the
  author on one machine. Reports from other players and Plasma versions are welcome.
